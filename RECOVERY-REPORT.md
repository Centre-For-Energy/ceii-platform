### Mission & Focus Areas

CEII operates as a multi-disciplinary energy advisory and investment facilitation organization. Its core activities span:

1. **Energy Investment Advisory** — Developing governance and investment frameworks for African governments (Nigeria, Liberia)
2. **Energy Transition Strategy** — Proposing the Nigeria Energy Transition Fund (NETFUND) and national decarbonization roadmaps
3. **Sovereign Fund Development** — Creating structured investment vehicles (New Niger Fund, New Liberia Fund/NLGIF)
4. **Human Capital Development** — Training programs, LMS platforms, learning frameworks
5. **Policy Advocacy** — Advising governments on energy policy, regulatory frameworks, and investor-friendly reforms
6. **Research & Publications** — OPEC bulletins, oil market analysis, energy sector reports
7. **International Partnerships** — SIDIL collaboration for decentralized energy, AFNIS summit participation

## Summary

CEII is a Nigerian-based energy advisory organization founded in 2021 by Grema Sulaiman Ahmad. It operates at the intersection of energy policy, investment facilitation, and governance consulting, with active projects in Nigeria and Liberia. The visual brand centers on a dark green (#024B38) and vibrant orange (#FF6200) palette with institutional typography. The legacy website (centreforenergy.org) was a WordPress-based TownPress theme with bilingual (EN/FR) support, featuring news aggregation, event management, document hosting, and a directory of energy summits.

The recovery yielded 21 archived HTML pages, 8 news articles, and comprehensive local assets including logos, proposals, roadmaps, and delegation documents. The primary gaps are: people profile pages, hosted images/PDFs, and the pre-2023 version of the site (which appears to have been a different, earlier iteration of the domain).
